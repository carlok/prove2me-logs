# The second size wave: a 1,550-line proof becomes 44

- **Mission** — Diaz's modulus conjecture, `3045e100-83a7-4863-9c02-21f90105f182`
- **Environment** — `0df444a` (Lean v4.33.1); mirrored on Mathlib v4.34.0
- **Date** — 2026-09-25

`DiazModulus.aligned_norm_free_no_rational_log_matrix` was the largest single
proof left in the library after Gelfond–Schneider: one submission of 1,550
lines. Tracing what its final theorem actually uses leaves 566 lines. The rest
was a block of results written for a different purpose and carried along.
About 170 of the 566 re-proved a node already on the board,
`det_zero_linear_forms_rank_one`.

## The tree

Carlo approved the map before anything was proved.

| node | lines | what it says |
|---|---|---|
| `Transcendence.quadratic_coeffs_eq_zero_of_transcendental` | 42 | a transcendental number is a root of no non-zero quadratic with algebraic coefficients |
| `DiazModulus.four_exp_barrier_of_no_quadratic_relation` | 38 | if numbers satisfy no rational quadratic relation, a singular 2×2 matrix of rational linear forms in them has rationally dependent rows or columns |
| `DiazModulus.aligned_norm_free_no_quadratic_relation` | 163 | in the aligned, norm-free case, `u`, `ū` and `2πi` satisfy no rational quadratic relation |

The first is used at `x = π` and `x = π²`. The second is the step that the
`*_four_exp_barrier` nodes had each carried inline. The third is the real
argument of the old proof, and it is where the hypothesis `|u|² ∉ ℚβ` does its
work. It no longer assumes that `π` is transcendental; it imports the node
that proves it.

## Four second proofs

| node | lines | now imports |
|---|---|---|
| `aligned_norm_free_no_rational_log_matrix` | 1,550 → 44 | the third node, the second |
| `period_free_split_nondegenerate` | 986 → 194 | the first, `pi_transcendental` |
| `recip_pi_log_four_exp_barrier` | 124 → 59 | the first two, `pi_transcendental` |
| `generic_conj_pair_four_exp_barrier` | 65 → 23 | the second, `generic_conj_pair_no_quadratic_relation` |

In all, 2,725 lines became 320, plus 243 in the three new nodes.

`generic_conj_pair_four_exp_barrier` was not in the original plan. It joined
because a node already on the board, `generic_conj_pair_no_quadratic_relation`,
states exactly the hypothesis of the second new node. Its proof is now one
application.

`period_free_split_nondegenerate` keeps its two explicit witnesses, both of
modulus 4. The first new node now does four of its steps, and about 700 lines
that the argument never used are gone.

## Checks before publishing

- On v4.33.1, all seven compile with no warnings. `sorryAx` enters only
  through the stubs of imported nodes.
- Each proof states its node exactly as published.
- On v4.34, chained against the mirror's real proofs, all seven use
  `propext`, `Classical.choice` and `Quot.sound` only.
- None of the three new statements was already on the board.

## On the board and in the mirror

The three statements published within a minute. The proofs went in three
waves: the first two nodes, then everything that needs only them, then the
44-line proof that needs the third. All seven were ACCEPTED at the first
attempt, with explanations attached and read back. Each new node's graph on
the board lists the proofs that import it.

`carlok/diaz-modulus-lean` `d09d7ad` counts 246 of 246: three new modules and
four regenerated. Three unrelated modules were regenerated as well.
- The porter renames a helper whose name also appears elsewhere in the
  library. Those three modules had helpers renamed only because the old
  1,550-line proof, and two others, declared the same names.
- With the old proofs gone, their names came back, and every module again
  matches its regeneration byte for byte.
