# Reading everything: attributions

- **Mission** — Diaz's modulus conjecture, `3045e100-83a7-4863-9c02-21f90105f182`
- **Environment** — `0df444a` (Lean v4.33.1); mirrored on Mathlib v4.34.0
- **Date** — 2026-09-24

After the morning's correction Carlo asked for everything available by Roy,
Waldschmidt, Diaz and the people around them to be read, books included, and
for the reading to look for ideas as well as priority. Nobody was contacted.

## What was read

About a hundred documents, legal copies only: Waldschmidt's *Diophantine
Approximation on Linear Algebraic Groups* (653 pages), his Lecture Notes 402
and the other lecture notes, several dozen of his papers from 1971 to 2026,
eight papers of Roy, Roy–Waldschmidt 1995 and 1997, Diaz 1997, 2004 and 2007,
Dasgupta 2023, Dasgupta–Kakde 2026 and Kirby. Scanned papers were run through
OCR, and every statement that mattered was checked on the page image. Seven
readers worked in parallel; the main session checked each finding it used at the
page. Not obtained: Roy's two J. Number Theory papers (1992, 2002), Brownawell
1974 and Roy's Acta Math. 1995 paper (publisher blocks), Waldschmidt's *Open
Diophantine problems* (2004), his Bourbaki talk 824, and a few others.

## What it found

No contradiction with any formal statement, and nothing that proves or refutes
an open one. What it did find is priority, and it is a lot:

- **Classical, or special cases of published results:** `e^{π²}` or `e^{iπ³}`
  (Waldschmidt 1973, Cor. 2; Brownawell 1974); `e^{i/π}` or `e^{iπ³}` and the
  other partners of (S) (Waldschmidt 1973, Cor. 4); the geometric triple
  (Lecture Notes 402, Cor. 7.4.3); the structure of the exceptional set of (S)
  (Diaz 2004, Théorèmes 4 and 5, at `v = −1/π`); the derivation from Schanuel
  (Diaz 2004, Prop. 1); the multiplier module, `u² ∉ ℒ̃` and the shifted
  reciprocal (Diaz 2007, Th. 4(1), Cor. 5; Waldschmidt 2005, Cor. 2.2); the
  involution lemma and the square duality (the ℚ-linear forms of Diaz 2007,
  Cor. 4(3) and 4(4)); the dimension count's no-go (Roy 1995, Th. 3.4).
- **Stronger known forms:** `2^{i log 2/π}` is transcendental as soon as `log 2`
  and `π` are algebraically dependent (Lecture Notes 402, p. 202); one of `2^π`,
  `3^π` is transcendental (the five exponentials theorem, 1988).
- **Attribution:** the strong four exponentials conjecture is Roy's (1992). The
  instantiation that derives Diaz's conjecture from it is Diaz's, recorded in
  Waldschmidt's book in 2000.
- **An error in the note's prose.** The boundary section said two things
  separate the available theory from Diaz's conjecture, the rank inequality and
  homogeneity. Over `ℒ̃` the constant 1 homogenises the conic, and a candidate
  violates the strict rank inequality on `H(u, r)` itself: the inequality alone
  would do. Homogeneity is the obstacle only on the transcendence-degree-one
  route over `ℒ`. The note also said the inhomogeneous direction was not being
  worked on; Waldschmidt proved a 2×3 inhomogeneous result in 2005 and
  conjectured the 2×2 case, which implies Diaz's.
- **False claims in node texts:** that a pair such as `2^π`, `3^π` is open;
  that no product of two logarithms is known to be transcendental (Diaz 2004,
  Th. 3, when exactly one factor lies on an axis); that the ℒ̃ form of six
  exponentials is an open conjecture (it is Roy's theorem); that the Schanuel
  derivation had never been written out. And two stale ones: the four
  exponentials node still called itself a citation boundary, and the mission
  root still listed it as an open leaf.

## For later

Statements worth porting, with sources and routes, are listed on the local side
in the mission's port list: among them the formal version of the `e^{|λ|}`
companion (Diaz 1997, Prop. 2), the Lecture Notes 402 strengthening of the
`log 2` result, and (S) at `γ = |u|²` for every candidate algebraic over `ℚ(π)`.
Each needs only the formalised four exponentials theorem. Nothing is published
until Carlo says so.

## The lesson

Seventeen node texts carried "possibly known; not checked against the
literature". The check found prior art for fourteen of them. The sentence was
honest, but the right time to check was before publishing.
