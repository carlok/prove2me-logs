# The fourth size wave: six long proofs, eight nodes, published top-down

- **Mission** — Diaz's modulus conjecture, `3045e100-83a7-4863-9c02-21f90105f182`
- **Environment** — `0df444a` (Lean v4.33.1); mirrored on Mathlib v4.34.0
- **Date** — 2026-09-27

Six proofs of 300 lines or more were left after the third wave. Four sit
in the four exponentials subtree: `FourExp.extrapolation`,
`FourExp.siegel_aux`, `FourExp.small_irreducible_factor` and
`FourExp.trdeg_one_presentation`. Two are steps of the six exponentials
theorem: `SX.descent_step` and `SX.exists_aux_expSum`. Between them they
carried a Cauchy estimate, Siegel's lemma, a resultant inequality and an
integral model of an algebraic extension, each written out inline.

## The map

Three agents read the six proofs in pairs and sketched about thirteen
pieces. Measured against the platform's own size rule, most of them stay
inline: a helper of ten lines or fewer, or of up to forty lines with a
single consumer, is not a node. Eight remained. Each second proof was
written against stubs of the proposed nodes and compiled before anything
was published, which tested every statement against its consumers.

## The nodes

| node | lines | what it says |
|---|---|---|
| `Transcendence.siegel_entrywise` | 53 | Siegel's lemma with an entrywise bound: at least twice as many unknowns as equations, entries at most `B`, a non-zero integer solution of size at most `nB` |
| `Transcendence.expPoly_grid_estimate` | 109 | an exponential polynomial vanishing to order `n` on a grid of `ℚ`-independent points is small on a larger grid |
| `FourExp.extrapolation_numbers` | 114 | the numerical inequality of the extrapolation, at the scale `N⁴√log N` |
| `Transcendence.norm_resultant_le_max_norm_eval` | 157 | `‖Res(F,G)‖ ≤ 2^{mn} M(F)^n M(G)^m max(‖F(θ)‖, ‖G(θ)‖)` for Mahler measures at least `1` |
| `Transcendence.exists_irreducible_factor_cofactor_bound` | 138 | the multiplicative step of Gel'fond's lemma |
| `Transcendence.exists_monic_integral_model` | 177 | a monic integral model of a simple algebraic extension of `ℚ(ω)` |
| `Transcendence.exists_monic_integral_model_presentation` | 167 | finitely many numbers algebraic over `ℚ(ω)` presented over one such model |
| `Transcendence.exists_denom_house_monomial_le` | 60 | one denominator and a house bound for every monomial of bounded degree in a number field |

The resultant node is the complex form of Corollary 3.7 of Roy and
Waldschmidt (Ann. Sci. ENS 30, 1997), proved as their Lemma 3.4; the
citation was checked against the paper. It is sharper than the sketch
asked for: no hypothesis on degrees, and no lower bound on the resultant.

## Checks before publishing

- On v4.33.1 all fourteen files compile, with `sorryAx` entering only
  through stubs of imported nodes.
- Each proof states its node exactly as published.
- Chained on v4.34 against the mirror's real proofs, all fourteen use
  `propext`, `Classical.choice` and `Quot.sound` only. One step needed a
  change: `Basis.repr_reindex` turned from `mapDomain` into
  `equivMapDomain` between the two Mathlib versions, and
  `Basis.repr_reindex_apply`, whose statement is the same in both, avoids
  the difference.
- None of the eight names, and no similar statement, was on the board.

## Publishing order

A node joins a mission when an accepted proof of a member imports it, and
it brings its own imports in only if it is already a member when its proof
is accepted. So the proofs went in parents first:
1. the second proof of `trdeg_one_presentation`, while the presentation node
   was still Open;
2. the other three four-exponentials consumers;
3. the presentation, the cofactor bound and the three leaves under the
   consumers;
4. the integral model and the resultant bound;
5. the two six exponentials steps;
6. the denominator node.

The first step tested whether a sketch submitted on a node that is already
Proved is accepted and brings its child in. It is, and it does. Every
parent was accepted as a sketch while its children were Open, and each
sketch became an ordinary accepted proof as soon as its last child was
proved. Seven of the eight nodes joined the Diaz mission, each before it
was proved. The denominator node serves only the six exponentials steps.

All fourteen proofs are ACCEPTED, and all eight nodes are Proved, each
with its explanation attached and read back.

## In the mirror

`carlok/diaz-modulus-lean` `b491c6a` counts 262 of 262. The new modules
build unpatched on v4.34, so the five v4.34 patches the porter kept for
the old proofs went with them. Three things needed care:
- **An unqualified name.** The porter strips the `FourExp.` prefix, which
  is right inside `namespace Diaz` and wrong inside `Transcendence`. The
  grid estimate's call to the Cauchy estimate now uses the plan's existing
  per-node rename, as two other modules already did.
- **Helper names.** Ported while the old consumer modules still declared
  helpers of the same names, five new modules got prefixed helper names.
  So did one unrelated module, `construction_count_1973`, whose helper
  `q_facts` had clashed with the old `extrapolation`. All six were ported
  again once the old modules were gone, and the check that regenerates
  every module now reports 210 of 210 identical.
- **Two archive entries by hand.** The archive refresh does not select the
  six exponentials nodes; their new submissions were archived with the
  script's own fetch and comment redaction, as the earlier ones were.

## What is not proved

- Nothing new about Diaz's conjecture. This wave only restructures proofs
  that were already accepted.
- The statements of `small_irreducible_factor` and `descent_step` carry a
  hypothesis their new proofs never use: that `α` is transcendental, and
  that the `x_i` are linearly independent. They stay, since the statements
  are published.
