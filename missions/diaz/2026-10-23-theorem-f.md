# Which conjugation hulls of a rational z carry a configuration
- **Mission** — Diaz's modulus conjecture, `3045e100-83a7-4863-9c02-21f90105f182`
- **Environment** — `0df444a` (Lean v4.33.1); mirrored on Mathlib v4.35.0
- **Date** — 2026-10-23

Roy's strong six exponentials theorem excludes a number z from ℒ̃ at a
candidate u as soon as the hull span(1, u, ū, z, z̄) carries a 2 × 3
configuration. Theorem E (R18) decided which hulls do when z is a
Laurent polynomial in u and ū. Its research note listed the next step:
every z rational in u. This round (R20) does that in 22 nodes.

## What was proved

Let u be transcendental with uū algebraic, and H₀ = span(1, u, u⁻¹).

- **Theorem NF.** Let W ⊇ H₀ be five-dimensional, and let a
  configuration in W have ratio p(u)/q(u) with p, q coprime of degree
  two. Then W is complete (D1), two-orbit (D2a) or cyclic (D2b), each an
  explicit span of H₀ and two rational functions. W is not assumed
  rational.
- **Theorem F.** For z rational in u, the hull carries a configuration
  exactly when it is four-dimensional and z lies in
  span(1, u, ū, 1/(u − a)) with aā = uū, or five-dimensional and of
  type D1, D2a or D2b. The five-dimensional part holds without
  conjugation, for any such space of rational functions.
- **Degree one.** D1 means: W contains u², u⁻² or some 1/(u − a), or
  W = H₀ + Q̄g + Q̄ug. Under conjugation the second case is a real
  pencil space W(λ, f).

## How

- **The classification was already in the project's notes** (R12,
  Theorem 2), proved there with rank-two vector bundles on P¹. Lean
  has no such bundles, so the round needed a proof without them. Two
  agents found one independently; the synthesis kept the shorter.
- **The proof is linear algebra.** Write h = p(u)/q(u) and J = p′q − pq′.
  The resultant of p and q is non-zero, so 1 and J(u)/q(u) are free
  over Q̄(h). Hence at most two values λ admit a pole fraction in W;
  one of λ = 0, 1, 2 does not, and for it the multiplier space of
  q/(p − λq) gives a complement U of H₀ in W. Multiplication on U is a
  2 × 2 matrix over Q̄: scalar, two eigenvalues, or a Jordan block.
  These are the three shapes.
- **Checks before Lean.** Two blind referees checked the design
  (correct with gaps; one Lean draft was false in characteristic 2 and
  got `CharZero`). Exact computation agreed with Theorem E's criterion
  on 141 Laurent hulls and ran the proof step by step on 1,047 spaces.
- **Lean.** Seven agents proved the 22 nodes in 22 minutes: 2,846
  lines, at most 256 content lines per file, each compiling in under
  41 seconds alone. Three unused hypotheses were dropped from the
  statements before publishing. No rejection.
- **Publishing.** One node (the conjugation-stable degree-one case) was
  neither a milestone nor imported by one, so its child could not join
  the mission by import. Both are now milestones (153, 154).

## What is not proved

- **Theorem F does not formally give Theorem E back.** For Laurent z,
  the agreement with Theorem E's classes is checked by computation
  only, and Theorem E's explicit criteria do not follow from it.
- **No exclusion at a candidate is stated.** At a candidate, every
  configuration found here excludes z from ℒ̃ by Roy's theorem; that
  step is a substitution and is not a node of this round.
- **Not formal:** that the type and the involution of a degree-two
  space are unique, the visibility criterion for degree-two spaces,
  and the explicit conjugation-stable families of the notes for a
  general involution.
- **Novelty.** The classification was not found in the sources read,
  but no source on scrolls or rank-two bundles on P¹ was read, and a
  geometer may call it routine. The dimension-four configuration is
  Diaz 2007, Théorème 7(1), with its exclusion in his Corollaire 5(4).
  Its tier-one place in the blueprint is editorial.
- **No step towards Diaz's conjecture.** It says exactly what Roy's
  theorem can exclude through one hull of a rational z.
