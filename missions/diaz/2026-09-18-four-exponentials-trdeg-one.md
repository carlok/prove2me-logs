# Four exponentials in transcendence degree one, machine-checked

- **Mission** — Diaz's modulus conjecture, `3045e100-83a7-4863-9c02-21f90105f182`
- **Environment** — Mathlib `0df444a` (Lean v4.33.1); also compiled on the
  mirror's v4.32.0
- **Date** — 2026-09-18
- **Nodes** — `FourExp.rank_one_parametrization`, `FourExp.construction_growth`
  and `FourExp.construction_count_1973`, Proved; then by cascade
  `FourExp.auxiliary_construction`,
  `FourExp.small_polynomials_of_counterexample`,
  `DiazModulus.four_exponentials_trdeg_one`, and
  `DiazModulus.diaz_of_exp_not_real_irrational_angle_period_aligned_norm_rat_mult`

The four exponentials theorem in transcendence degree one is proved. If
`x₁, x₂` and `y₁, y₂` are two pairs of ℚ-linearly independent complex
numbers whose four products have algebraic exponentials, then the field
generated by the `xᵢ` and `yⱼ` has transcendence degree at least two. Every
step, from Siegel's lemma to the final contradiction, is now checked by
Lean.

## What was proved

Three small leaves closed the 1973 construction:

| Theorem | Node | Submission |
|---|---|---|
| `FourExp.rank_one_parametrization` | `30f831f0` | `8517773b` |
| `FourExp.construction_growth` | `756ef0b1` | `c2ad50d4` |
| `FourExp.construction_count_1973` | `fede9f8c` | `90439cea` |

Each was accepted on its first submission. The cascade then closed four
nodes above them, including `DiazModulus.four_exponentials_trdeg_one` and the
half of a Diaz branch that reduces to it.

- **Rank-one parametrization.** A rank-one matrix of logarithms, no row or
  column relation over ℚ, becomes an outer product `xᵢyⱼ` with both pairs
  independent, without raising the transcendence degree.
- **Growth.** The two majorants `x²√log x` and `x²/√log x`, continued
  affinely below `3`, are strictly increasing, tend to infinity, are ordered,
  and satisfy `f(x+1) ≤ 3f(x)`.
- **Counting.** The number of Siegel equations stays below the number of
  unknowns for large `N`, floors and fractional powers included.

## How

**Rank one.** Take `x = (l₁₁, l₂₁)` and `y = (1, l₁₂/l₁₁)`. The rank-one
relation makes the four products the four logarithms. Independence of each
pair is exactly the two non-degeneracy hypotheses. The transcendence degree
is the only delicate point, because the ratio need not lie in the ring
generated by the logarithms. No transcendental element has to be named: the
ratio is algebraic over that ring, because `l₁₁X − l₁₂` kills it and `l₁₁`
is non-zero, so additivity of transcendence degree in a tower (Stacks 030H,
in Mathlib as `trdeg_add_eq`) gives the bound.

**Growth.** Three numerical facts carry it: `1 < log 3 < 2` and
`log 4 < 1.4`. The only non-obvious monotonicity is that of `x²/√log x`,
which follows from `log y / y ≤ log x / x` past `e`, i.e. Mathlib's
`Real.log_div_self_antitoneOn`.

**Counting.** The leading terms are `80N⁴/√log N` against `98N⁴/√log N`, so
the margin is real but not generous; the floors cost only `O(N³)`. The
remainder term, which carries `U^{1/20}` and `1/log U`, is beaten by the
growth of `√log N` alone, so the crude bound `U^{1/20} ≤ N` suffices.

Two Lean lessons, both about tactics rather than mathematics. `linarith`
treats `18 / s` and `9 / s` as unrelated atoms when `s` is not a numeral, so
divisions by a root must be replaced by bounded quantities first. And inside
a proof with a large context, `nlinarith` and `positivity` become unusable,
because they preprocess every hypothesis: each numeric step belongs in its
own small lemma.

The GitHub library now holds 164 of 164 results with only Lean's three
axioms, CI green.

## What is not proved

Diaz's modulus conjecture itself is still open, and so is the branch that
needs more than the four-exponentials input. What closed here is the
transcendence-degree-one case of the four exponentials theorem, along the
route of Waldschmidt 1973 and 1971, and the one Diaz branch that reduces to
it. The statement is the degree-one case, not the full four exponentials
conjecture. The parameters are the paper's fixed choices, and the constants
are explicit but far from optimal.

## What remains open

Under `DiazModulus.diaz_modulus_conjecture`, the open frontier is now the
non-real-exponential branch: off both axes, irrational angle, and the
period-aligned node above the half that just closed. The old non-1973 and
non-algebraic variants of the construction nodes stay on the board as a
documented dead branch.
