# The four exponentials conjecture sees nothing homogeneous

- **Mission** — Diaz's modulus conjecture, `3045e100-83a7-4863-9c02-21f90105f182`
- **Environment** — `0df444a` (Lean v4.33.1); mirrored on Mathlib v4.34.0
- **Date** — 2026-09-24

The companion note used to end on an informal remark: the four
exponentials theorem, and even the four exponentials conjecture, needs
its products to be logarithms of algebraic numbers, and only the strong
form, whose products may range over the algebraic span, reaches Diaz's
problem. This entry records a theorem that makes the remark precise,
and sharpens it.

## What was proved

| node | inputs |
|---|---|
| `det_zero_linear_forms_rank_one` | none |
| `generic_conj_pair_no_quadratic_relation` | none |
| `generic_conj_pair_four_exp_barrier` | the two above |
| `candidate_nongeneric_four_exp_barrier` | 4E₁ |
| `recip_pi_log_four_exp_barrier` | transcendence of π, the first node |

A candidate certifies three logarithms of algebraic numbers: `u`, `ū`
and `iπ`. Every singular 2×2 matrix whose entries are rational
combinations of them has ℚ-dependent rows or columns. On that data the
four exponentials conjecture has no instance: it is already a theorem.

- For a generic candidate, meaning `u` and `π` are algebraically
  independent, this is pure algebra. It does not even use `e^u`: no
  rational quadratic form vanishes at `(u, ū, iπ)`, and a 2×2 matrix of
  linear forms with vanishing determinant has dependent rows or
  columns.
- For a candidate algebraic over `ℚ(π)` it is the proved four
  exponentials theorem in transcendence degree one.

The same holds for the two logarithms an exception to (S) would
certify, `γ/(iπ)` and `iπ`.

## How

The key identity: substitute `ū = |u|²/u` into a quadratic form in
`(u, ū, iπ)` and multiply by `u²`. The result is a polynomial in `u` and
`iπ` with six distinct monomials, `u⁴, u³iπ, u²(iπ)², u², u·iπ, 1`, and
algebraic independence kills every coefficient. The Lean proof never
reads off coefficients. It evaluates the vanishing polynomial at six
integer points instead.

The linear-forms lemma is classical, usually proved by unique
factorisation in the polynomial ring. The formal proof is a coordinate
argument closed by `linear_combination`.

## What is not proved

This is a barrier, not progress on the conjecture: it says which tools
cannot work. The research agent's report also:

- characterises the homogeneous relations a non-generic candidate can
  carry, `Q(Im u, π) = s|u|²` with `Q` a rational binary quadratic form;
- shows that the anisotropic ones, such as `Re(u²) = π²`, survive every
  proved theorem but never give a 2×2 configuration.

Neither of those is formalised. Nor is the observation that in the
generic case the period `iπ` never enters a configuration that detects
a candidate.

A small correction to the note came out of the same round. Version 1.2
offered `r·e^i` as a point "certainly not" a candidate. Whether it is
one is itself an instance of the conjecture; a non-candidate is found
by countability.

## What remains open

Unchanged: the real half of (S), and (NT). What would reach them is a
statement whose matrices carry a constant entry, the strong four
exponentials conjecture or something of that inhomogeneous kind.

**Correction (later the same day).** The anisotropic relations do not
survive every proved theorem. Théorème 0.2 of Roy–Waldschmidt (Ann. Sci.
École Norm. Sup. 30, 1997), the quadric version of the four exponentials
theorem in transcendence degree one, excludes every rational quadratic
relation among `u`, `ū`, `iπ` for a candidate algebraic over `ℚ(π)` with
`Im u ∉ ℚπ`. So no such candidate carries one. The formal results stand;
the texts that said otherwise were corrected. See
[the literature check](2026-09-24-literature-check.md).
