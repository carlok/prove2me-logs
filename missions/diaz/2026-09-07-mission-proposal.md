# Schanuel as a definition makes two Diaz milestones provable today

- **Mission** — Diaz's modulus conjecture,
  `039adfb9-57cd-4cef-a6f4-c59dcf005f77` (status `Draft`)
- **Environment** — `0df444a3` (Lean v4.33.1)
- **Date** — 2026-09-07

Write `Q̄` for the algebraic numbers inside `ℂ`, and `ℒ` for the set of
`u ∈ ℂ` whose exponential `eᵘ` is algebraic. In 2004 Guy Diaz asked
whether any non-zero element of `ℒ` can have algebraic modulus, and
conjectured that none can. The statement needs nothing beyond `exp` and
`|·|`, and it has been open for twenty-two years. Diaz records in one
sentence that it follows from Schanuel's conjecture and also from the
strong four exponentials conjecture, so it sits underneath two standard
pillars of transcendence theory while being far more concrete than
either. This entry records the mission proposal built around that
sentence. It records no progress on the conjecture itself.

## What was proved

Nothing about the conjecture. What is stable is the proposal: a
nine-item ladder, titled *Diaz's modulus conjecture: if |u| is
algebraic, e^u is transcendental*, whose definition bundle
`Def_DiazModulus.lean` compiles with no errors and no `sorry`, and every
one of whose milestone statements elaborates. Each item was compiled in
isolation against the exact preamble the platform uses, because one
draft that fails to compile fails the whole Submit.

Items, in `item_order`; five are marked as milestones.

1. `DiazModulus` — the definition bundle
2. `DiazModulus.logAlg_conj_stable`
3. `DiazModulus.conj_eq_norm_sq_div`
4. `DiazModulus.hermite_lindemann_holds` — milestone
5. `DiazModulus.six_exponentials` — milestone
6. `DiazModulus.diaz_on_axes_of_hermite_lindemann` — milestone
7. `DiazModulus.diaz_of_schanuel` — milestone
8. `DiazModulus.diaz_of_strongFourExponentials_and_hermite_lindemann`
   — milestone
9. `DiazModulus.diaz_modulus_conjecture` — the goal

The goal is `DiazModulus.DiazModulusConjecture`: for every `u ≠ 0` such
that `((‖u‖ : ℝ) : ℂ)` is algebraic over `ℚ`, `Complex.exp u` is
transcendental.

## How

The design decision worth explaining is that `HermiteLindemann`,
`SchanuelConjecture` and `StrongFourExponentials` are `Prop`-valued
**definitions** in the bundle, not axioms. Nothing is assumed silently;
a statement that needs one of them carries it as a hypothesis and says
so in its name.

That choice is what turns Diaz's one-sentence remark into work. Two
milestones become conditional theorems — `diaz_of_schanuel` and
`diaz_of_strongFourExponentials_and_hermite_lindemann` — with no open
input at all, so they are provable today. They are also the real
mathematical content of the remark, which the literature never writes
out.

`diaz_of_schanuel` splits on whether `u` and `conj u` are
`ℚ`-independent. If they are, Schanuel at `n = 2` with
`x = ![u, conj u]` forces `trdeg ℚ` of the field generated by
`u, conj u, eᵘ, e^{conj u}` to be at least `2`; but `eᵘ` is algebraic by
hypothesis, `e^{conj u} = conj (eᵘ)` is algebraic by item 2, and
`conj u = |u|²/u` by item 3, so everything sits inside `Q̄(u)`, of
transcendence degree at most `1`. If they are dependent then
`conj u = q·u` with `q = ±1`, putting `u` on an axis, and Schanuel at
`n = 1` yields Hermite–Lindemann outright, so no separate
`HermiteLindemann` hypothesis is needed in this milestone.

`diaz_of_strongFourExponentials_and_hermite_lindemann` is the sharper of
the two and never touches transcendence degree. Set `λ = ‖u‖` and take
`x₁ = u`, `x₂ = λ`, `y₁ = 1`, `y₂ = λ/u`. The four products are `u`,
`λ`, `λ` and `λ²/u = conj u`, and all four lie in `ℒ̃`, the `Q̄`-span of
`{1} ∪ ℒ`. Both pairs are `Q̄`-linearly independent precisely because
`u ∉ Q̄`, which is Hermite–Lindemann applied to the hypothesis that `eᵘ`
is algebraic. The configuration contradicts the conjecture.

Hermite–Lindemann is not merely an input to that argument. It is a
**special case of the goal**: if `a ≠ 0` is algebraic then
`|a|² = a · conj a` is algebraic, hence `|a|` is algebraic, and the goal
at `u := a` gives `Complex.exp a` transcendental. So Diaz is strictly
stronger than Hermite–Lindemann, no route to the goal can skip that
node, and milestone 8 assuming it is a weakening rather than
circularity. That observation came out of a blind read-back audit, not
out of drafting.

Seven auditor runs, each given only the Lean and an auditor reference,
changed four other things. A rename had replaced `theorem NAME ` with a
trailing space, but the declaration wraps onto the next line, so the
substitution silently did nothing and the item's `theorem_name`
disagreed with the declaration; **no build catches this**, since a Lean
module name is unrelated to the declaration inside it, and it would have
failed Submit. Schanuel was stated over `Algebra.adjoin`, the generated
subalgebra, where the classical statement wants the generated field —
not a change of strength, since `trdeg` of a domain equals that of its
fraction field, but a moderator should not have to know that; it is now
`IntermediateField.adjoin`. `candidate_conj_eq` carried a `u ≠ 0` it did
not need. And two names concealed the `HermiteLindemann` argument they
took, which is why items 6 and 8 have the names they do.

## What is not proved

No case of Diaz's conjecture. Schanuel, the strong four exponentials
conjecture and Hermite–Lindemann are all carried as hypotheses; none is
discharged, and the two conditional milestones establish implications
rather than results.

Milestone 6, `diaz_on_axes_of_hermite_lindemann`, has no force as a
statement about moduli. On either axis "modulus algebraic" is
*equivalent* to "`u` algebraic", so the milestone is an instance of the
assumed Hermite–Lindemann rather than an application of it. This was
kept rather than fixed, and said plainly in the item itself.

Hermite–Lindemann is genuinely absent from the platform's Mathlib. Only
`NumberTheory/Transcendental/Lindemann/AnalyticalPart` is present;
`leanprover-community/mathlib4#28013` has been open since 2025-08-05 and
was `awaiting-author` on 2026-09-07. Absence was checked against all
three pinned revisions: none contains `transcendental_exp`.

The proposal is a `Draft`. It carries a read-back from an independent
blind auditor on all nine items, and it awaits a human Submit.

## What remains open

Item 9, the goal, is open and stays open; anything closing it closes a
case of both conjectures above.

Milestones 7 and 8 are closable today. Milestone 7 is the more expensive
of the two: `Algebra.trdeg` is cardinal-valued and has almost no
computational API, so the proof needs a lemma of the shape "if `S ⊆ K(t)`
with `t` transcendental over `K` then `trdeg K (adjoin K S) ≤ 1`". That
lemma is worth publishing as a child in its own right; it is reusable
well beyond this mission.

What a solver should not try is recorded on the platform as a formalized
negative result. Because `conj u = ρ/u` with `ρ = |u|²` algebraic,
complex conjugation on `Q̄(u)` is a rational function of the generator
rather than independent data, so accumulating algebraic relations
between `u` and `ū` until they collide cannot separate a candidate from
an ordinary transcendental point on the same circle. Any successful
attack has to introduce information that is not a rational function of
`u` over `Q̄` — which is the methodological question Diaz raised on
p. 551 and left open.
