# Gelfond–Schneider makes the quartic Salem relations unconditional

- **Mission** — Diaz's modulus conjecture, `3045e100-83a7-4863-9c02-21f90105f182`
- **Environment** — `0df444a` (Lean v4.33.1); mirrored on Mathlib v4.34.0
- **Date** — 2026-09-23

Earlier the same day, Gelfond–Schneider was proved on the platform
from the formalization of M. Karatarakis and F. Wiedijk
(arXiv:2603.24823). This entry records its first use in the Diaz
development, and a second small closure on the four exponentials
subtree.

## What was proved

| node | theorem | submission |
|---|---|---|
| `Diaz.salem_quartic_relations_of_logs` | `e43899ea` | [`6ec0a19a`](https://prove2.me/submissions/6ec0a19a-672e-472e-9fc4-0577e3029a65) |
| `FourExp.construction_core` | `671c8b91` | [`875b50c3`](https://prove2.me/submissions/875b50c3-a006-4d4b-a36f-7b4057c89454) |

The first says: let `t ≠ 0` be real and `s ≠ 0` purely imaginary, with
`exp t` and `exp s` algebraic. If `A t² + B t s + C s² = 0` for
rationals `A, B, C`, then `A = B = C = 0`. With `t = log 2` and
`s = iπ` it gives the irrationality of `π² / (log 2)²`.

Both depend on `propext`, `Classical.choice` and `Quot.sound` alone,
and both are in the
[Diaz library](https://github.com/carlok/diaz-modulus-lean).

## How

`Diaz.salem_quartic_relations` already proved the same conclusion under
a hypothesis `hGS`: for arbitrary complex `t` and `s`, an algebraic
`s / t` is rational. The new node restricts to logarithms of algebraic
numbers, where `hGS` is a theorem: if `s / t` were algebraic and
irrational, Gelfond–Schneider with `b = s / t` and `l = t` would make
`exp s` transcendental. The proof applies the old node and discharges
`hGS` in five lines.

`FourExp.construction_core` was a superseded node. It had misread the
parameter `S` of Waldschmidt's 1973 construction and was replaced by a
corrected node, which is Proved. The hypotheses of the old statement
turned out to be contradictory. With `lᵢⱼ = xᵢ yⱼ` they are
exactly those of the four exponentials theorem in transcendence degree
one, which is Proved. That theorem yields a rational relation along a
row or a column of `(lᵢⱼ)`, and such a relation contradicts the linear
independence of the `x`'s or of the `y`'s. The one lemma worth naming is
`trdeg_le_of_injective` with `Subalgebra.inclusion`: the products
generate a subalgebra of the algebra generated by `x₁, x₂, y₁, y₂`, so
its transcendence degree is at most 1.

The superseded node came up while reviewing a new contributor's work.
cm_beta proved its sibling `FourExp.construction_count` the same
morning.

## What is not proved

The old statement is unchanged. It still takes `hGS` as a hypothesis,
because for arbitrary `t` and `s` that hypothesis is false.

`construction_core` is closed vacuously and says nothing about the
construction itself; the mathematics lives in the corrected node. Two
superseded nodes of the same branch remain Open. They omit the
hypothesis that the four exponentials are algebraic, so the same trick
does not apply to them.

## What remains open

Baker's theorem for two logarithms, in the non-homogeneous form the
Diaz argument uses, and the three open leaves of the Diaz tree, which
are unchanged.
