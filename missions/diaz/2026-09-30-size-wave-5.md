# The fifth size wave: six proofs that re-proved what already existed

- **Mission** — Diaz's modulus conjecture, `3045e100-83a7-4863-9c02-21f90105f182`
- **Environment** — `0df444a` (Lean v4.33.1); mirrored on Mathlib v4.34.0
- **Date** — 2026-09-30

After the fourth wave the audit listed eight proofs of 300 lines or more.
Two of them were never long. The audit had counted old submissions still
kept in the archive, while the mirror ports the short ones that replaced
them. Measured on the mirror, six remained, plus one at exactly 300.

## The map

Three agents read the proofs and measured candidate nodes against the
size rules: a helper of ten lines or fewer stays inline, one of up to
forty lines becomes a node only with two consumers, and a proof between
300 and 399 lines gives up only its standalone helpers over forty.
Every second proof was compiled in scratch before anything else happened.

What they found was mostly repetition, not structure:

| proof | lines | what the length was |
|---|---|---|
| `…_period_aligned_norm_rat_mult` | 420 → 99 | about 150 lines re-proved the published non-generic four exponentials barrier |
| `transfer_breaks_exactly` | 413 → 84 | 183 lines that no theorem used, and a Mathlib lemma written out by hand |
| `transcendence_criterion_continuous` | 399 → 245 | a height API that is Mathlib's `Polynomial.supNorm` |
| `construction_growth` | 344 → 191 | one argument written twice, and Mathlib's gluing of monotone pieces |
| `expPoly_value_le_derivs` | 336 → 65 | binomial bookkeeping that a closed-form bound replaces |
| `GelfondSchneider.deriv_upper` | 322 → 200 | the fourth wave's grid estimate, re-proved inline |

The last row settles a guess left by the fourth wave, which expected the
grid estimate to save 50 to 75 lines in `deriv_upper`. Measured, it saves
103 lines of code.

One node is new. `Transcendence.expPoly_iteratedDeriv_le` bounds every
derivative of an exponential polynomial at a point by its first `N` there:
`‖f⁽ⁿ⁾(c)‖ ≤ D(W+1)^{n+N}`. The proof peels one frequency at a time. Its
page claims no source and no novelty. It is the elementary induction along
the differential equation such a function satisfies.

## What was left out, and why

- `construction_count_1973` would have dropped from 300 lines to 127, but
  only by compressing tactics. The resubmission rule asks for duplicated or
  re-proved lines, so it waits.
- Two short helpers with a single consumer each stay inline.
- The dead lemmas of `transfer_breaks_exactly` were dropped, not kept
  elsewhere. They remain on record in the old accepted submission.

## Publication and the mirror

The node went up first, and the second proof of its consumer was
submitted while the node was still Open. It was accepted as a sketch, and
the node joined the mission by import. Then the node's proof, then the
five other second proofs: all accepted, with their explanations read back.

Porting into the mirror hit the fourth wave's trap once more. Dropping the
dead code removed a helper name, so twelve other modules would now be
generated with that helper unprefixed. They were re-ported, and the porter's
own verification reads 216 identical, 0 differing.

The six proofs went from 2,234 lines to 884 in the mirror, 998 with the
node.
